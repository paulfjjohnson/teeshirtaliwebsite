# Running a Tee Party

A Tee Party is a timed, exclusive design drop. During the party, its designs appear **only on the party page** (on your drop schedule, with party discounts). When it closes, those designs are released into the linked store's Design Library at regular price.

## Creating a party

A Tee Party is a WordPress **Page** using the **Tee Party** template. Create a page, assign that template, and fill in the **Tee Party Settings** box.

## Tee Party Settings

- **Active** — turns on the live status, countdown, and revealed designs on the front end.
- **Party Name** — e.g. "Summer Spirit Drop 2026".
- **School / Store** — link the party to a store. The configurator then runs *in that store* (its colors, cart routing, and discount), and on close the designs release into that store's library. Leave as *General* for a non-store party.
- **Colors** — the party's brand colors (accent, highlight, background, text).
- **Apparel** — *Full TSA Catalog* or *Specific Garments Only*, with per-garment color limits (same rules as a store: none checked = all colors).
- **Quantity Discounts** — "buy this many → this % off" tiers shown on the party page.
- **Schedule / Drops** — pick designs and a **reveal time** for each. Designs appear on the party page as their drop time arrives.

## The lifecycle

1. **Live** — the countdown runs; designs reveal on schedule; customers order with party discounts. The designs are **not** in the store library yet — that's what keeps the drop exclusive.
2. **Grace** — the deadline passes but checkout stays open briefly.
3. **Closed** — the party ends. Its designs are **released into the linked store** (store-exclusive, at regular price). The closed party page's cards, and a hero button, now point customers to **"Shop the {Store} Store"** so traffic flows to the permanent store.

## Releasing early

In the settings box, **"Release designs to library now"** pushes the designs into the store immediately without waiting for the close. Re-running it also re-syncs the tags.
