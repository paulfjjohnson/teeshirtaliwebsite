# Stores: Apparel, Colors & Drops

Every store — school, team, or business — is a "permanent Tee Party": you choose the apparel it offers, the colors for each garment, and you can schedule designs to drop into it over time. It all lives on the **store record**.

## Opening a store

Command Center → **Stores** (or wp-admin → Stores). Open the store you want to edit. You'll see several panels; the two that control the shopping experience are **Store Apparel & Colors** and **Store Design Drops**.

## Store Apparel & Colors

This sets what customers can pick when they configure a design **in this store**.

- **Full TSA Catalog** — the store offers every active garment.
- **Specific Garments Only** — check exactly which garments this store carries. For each checked garment you can also limit the colors:

> Leave **every** color unchecked for a garment to allow **all** of its colors. Check some to restrict to just those.

Example: Dutchtown offers a tee and a hoodie, each limited to Purple / Gray / Black. Save, and the configurator for that store shows only those garments and colors.

## Store Design Drops

Schedule designs to go live in the store — the same idea as a Tee Party drop, but permanent.

- Add a row: pick a **Design** and a **Reveal date/time**.
- **Leave the date blank** to drop it live immediately.
- A future date makes it appear automatically at that time.

When a drop's time hits, the design is tagged to the store, published, and becomes orderable — it shows up in the store's program grid and its configurator. No further action needed.

## How designs reach a store

A design belongs to a store via its **Store** tag (who owns it) and is grouped by its **Category** (what it is — Color Guard, Cheer, etc.). Designs scheduled here, or released from a closed Tee Party, are tagged to the store automatically. See the **Design Library** article for the full tagging model.

## The storefront

Customers browse at the store's pages (e.g. `/schools/<store>/`): a branded home, a **Programs hub** (category cards → that program's designs), and each design links into the **store-branded configurator** with the apparel and colors you set above.
