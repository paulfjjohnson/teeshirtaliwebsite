# Design Library

The Design Library is your catalog of reusable artwork. Every design is described **two ways at once**, and keeping these two ideas separate is the key to running many stores without extra work.

## The two things every design has

- **Design Categories** — *what the design is* (the kind of art). One shared, global list that every store reuses. Example: `By Sport → Football`.
- **Store** — *who owns it* (which school, team, or business). Example: `dutchtown`.

A store page shows a design by filtering on the **Store**, then groups what it finds under the shared **Categories**. That means you never create categories per school — you reuse the same category list everywhere.

> **Rule of thumb:** Category = *what it is*. Store = *who owns it*. Never name a category after a school — that's the Store's job.

## Worked example

Say you upload four designs for **Dutchtown**. You don't create any Dutchtown categories — you set the **same Store** on each, and pick *what it is* from the categories that already exist:

| Design | Store (who owns it) | Design Categories (what it is) |
|--------|---------------------|--------------------------------|
| Dutchtown Football 2026 | dutchtown · School | By Sport → Football, Team Gear → Game Day |
| Homecoming Spirit Tee | dutchtown · School | School Events → Homecoming, School Events → Spirit Week |
| NHS Induction Shirt | dutchtown · School | Student Organizations → National Honor Society (NHS) |
| "Go Cats" Wordmark | dutchtown · School | General → Typography / Wordmark |

The Dutchtown store page then **auto-groups** those four under the headings **Football / Homecoming / NHS / Typography**. No Dutchtown-specific category was ever created.

### Adding a second school — zero category overhead

To onboard **Walker**, create **one** new Store (`walker`, type School) and tag Walker's designs with the **same** shared categories:

| Design | Store | Design Categories |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Walker Football Game Day | walker · School | By Sport → Football, Team Gear → Game Day |
| Walker Homecoming | walker · School | School Events → Homecoming |

`Football` and `Homecoming` are the **same** category terms Dutchtown used — reused, not duplicated. Walker's page shows only Walker's designs because the filter is the **Store**, not the category. Fifty schools still means the same handful of categories, not hundreds.

## Uploading a design

1. Go to **Designs → Add New**.
2. Give it a clear **title** (this shows to shoppers).
3. In **Design Categories**, check what the art *is* (one or more).
4. In the **Store** box, set the store slug (e.g. `dutchtown`) and the **Store Type**.
5. Upload the **hi-res print file** and a **preview image**.
6. Optionally mark **Featured** to surface it on the homepage.
7. **Publish.**

## Featured designs

Checking **Featured** adds a design to the homepage "Shop Featured Designs" area, grouped by its category. Use it to spotlight your best sellers and seasonal drops.

## Need a brand-new kind of art?

If a design genuinely doesn't fit any existing category (say, "Esports"), add that category **once** at the top level. Every store can then use it. Resist the urge to add it under a single school — that's exactly the duplication the Store tag exists to prevent.
