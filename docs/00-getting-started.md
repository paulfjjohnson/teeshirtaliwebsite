# Getting Started

Welcome to your Tee Shirt Ali platform. This Help Center explains every part of the system from the **store owner's** side — how to set things up, run them day to day, and avoid the common gotchas.

## What's included

- **Stores** — School, Team, and Business storefronts, each with its own designs and delivery rules.
- **Design Library** — your catalog of reusable designs, organized by category and owned by a store.
- **Tee Parties** — scheduled design "drops" with live reveals and per-garment color control.
- **Apparel Configurator** — the live mockup + pricing tool your customers use to customize products.
- **Fundraisers** — zero-risk campaigns with built-in reporting.
- **Gang Sheet Builder** — DTF gang-sheet ordering with per-width pricing.
- **Owner Command Center** — your dashboard for sales, orders, and reporting.
- **Form Email Routing** — control exactly where each form's submissions are sent.

## How to use this Help Center

Pick a topic from the list on the left, or use the **search box** to jump straight to a feature. Each article is a self-contained guide. You can open this same Help Center on your live site at `/help/` (manager login required) or here in the admin under **TSA Help**.

## The one habit that saves you headaches

After you change a setting, a price, or page content and **don't see it update live**, your site's page cache is probably serving an old copy. Clear it:

> **LiteSpeed Cache → Purge All.** Then hard-refresh the page (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R).

Most "it didn't work" moments are just caching. Purge first before assuming something is broken.
